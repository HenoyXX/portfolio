# AI LAB — Artificial Intelligence Research

**Project Type:** AI Research / Experimental Development  
**Status:** Planned

## Overview

AI LAB is a planned personal research environment focused on exploring artificial intelligence technologies, intelligent agents, automation systems, and emerging AI development tools.

The purpose of this project is to provide a structured space for learning, experimentation, and evaluating different approaches to building practical AI-powered solutions.

## Research Focus

### 1. AI Models
- Explore different language models.
- Compare local and cloud-based AI solutions.
- Understand model capabilities, limitations, and performance.

### 2. AI Agents
- Study autonomous and semi-autonomous AI agents.
- Explore tool calling and multi-step task execution.
- Investigate agent architectures and orchestration.

### 3. AI Automation
- Explore workflow automation using AI.
- Investigate connections between AI models and external tools.
- Study ways to reduce repetitive manual tasks.

### 4. Local AI Systems
- Research local model deployment.
- Explore hardware requirements and performance optimization.
- Investigate privacy-focused AI architectures.

### 5. AI Development Tools
- Evaluate frameworks, libraries, and development environments.
- Explore practical approaches to building AI applications.
- Document useful findings and technical lessons.

## Objectives

The main objectives are to:

- Build a stronger understanding of modern AI systems.
- Develop practical knowledge of AI agents and automation.
- Evaluate different technologies through structured research.
- Identify useful approaches for future projects.
- Apply relevant findings to projects such as PATCH and HENOYX where appropriate.

## Planned Workflow

1. Identify a research topic.
2. Review relevant technologies and documentation.
3. Evaluate available approaches.
4. Conduct practical experiments when ready.
5. Document observations and findings.
6. Identify potential real-world applications.
